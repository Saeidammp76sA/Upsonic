# Acme Analytics — Product Overview (Internal)

Acme Analytics is a B2B SaaS platform for real-time business intelligence.

## Pricing (effective 2026-01-01)
- Starter plan: $49/month per seat, 5 dashboards, 30-day data retention.
- Pro plan: $120/month per seat, unlimited dashboards, 1-year retention, API access.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, on-prem deployment, SLA of 99.95% uptime.
- Annual billing discount: 15% on all plans.

## Key policies
- Free trial lasts 14 days; no credit card required.
- Data residency options: EU (Frankfurt) and US (Virginia).
- Support response times: Starter 24h, Pro 8h, Enterprise 1h.

## FY2025 financial highlights
- ARR at end of FY2025: $18.4M (up 46% YoY).
- Gross margin: 78%. Net revenue retention: 112%.
- Churn (logo): 1.9% annually.
